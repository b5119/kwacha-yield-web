Kwacha Yield

A prototype that shows how a retail investor could sell Treasury Bills and Government Bonds before maturity. All banks and exchanges in it are simulated. No real trading or payments.

Android

Download the app (APK)
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source (a test build, not from the Play Store).
  3. Open Kwacha Yield and create an account.

36 MB. For 64-bit Android phones running Android 7 or newer, which covers almost every phone made since 2017.

iPhone and everything else

Open the web version

On an iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The web version has a PIN but no fingerprint or Face ID.

This is a student project prototype. Figures are estimates for illustration and are not financial advice.
